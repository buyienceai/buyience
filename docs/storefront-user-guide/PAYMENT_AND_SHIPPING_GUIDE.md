# Payment and Shipping Guide

**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. Important note

Your supplier’s store takes payment with **their** enabled methods. Buyience does not charge you separately. Only options shown on your checkout screen are real for your account.

---

## 2. Shipping (step by step)

**Where:** Checkout, after you choose a shipping address.

1. Select or enter a shipping address.  
2. Wait for shipping options to load for that address.  
3. Choose a method from the list (names come from your supplier / carriers).  
4. Check shipping and tax on the order summary when shown.

If no methods appear, fix the address or contact the store via **Support** / footer **Contact Us**. You may also see **Shipping Not Available**.

---

## 3. Payment for guests (B2C)

When enabled, checkout may show:

- **Credit Card** / **Credit / debit card**  
- **PayPal**  
- **Razorpay**

Methods that are not enabled simply do not appear.

---

## 4. Payment when signed in (B2B)

1. Choose **Payment terms** (required).  
2. Choose **Payment method** (required).  
3. Follow any on-screen credit or offline instructions.  
4. Note any message that payment is charged in the store’s base currency.

Examples you may see:

- **Credit / debit card**, **PayPal**, **Razorpay**  
- **Pay by invoice (no payment now)**  
- **Pay online later, from your invoice**  
- Other offline methods with your supplier’s own labels  

---

## 5. After payment

| Outcome | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| Success | Read **Order Placed Successfully!** and continue with **View Orders** or **Continue Shopping** |
| Failed | On **Payment Failed**, use **Try Again** or return to orders / shopping |
| Pay later link | Open the invoice pay page — **Balance due** — then pay or **Download** |
