# Buyience Storefront User Guide

**Who this is for:** Buyers and customer-account users of a Buyience-powered storefront (the shop / buyer portal your supplier gives you).

**What this is:** A practical how-to for customers — not seller/admin documentation, and not developer documentation.

**Last updated:** November 2026

---

## Important notes

| Topic | What it means for you |
|-------|------------------------|
| **Store branding** | Logo, colours, menus, homepage blocks, and footer links are set by your supplier. Button names in these guides match the real storefront labels. |
| **B2B vs B2C** | Some buttons (for example **Request Quote**) change by store type and whether you are signed in. |
| **Prices** | Some stores hide prices until you sign in (**Price on Request**). Your negotiated price may show as **Your Price**. |
| **Payment methods** | Only methods your supplier enabled appear (for example Credit Card, PayPal, Razorpay, or pay later). Do not assume every method exists. |
| **Coming Soon** | In the buyer **Dashboard**, some items such as **Purchase Orders** may show **Coming Soon** and are not available yet. |
