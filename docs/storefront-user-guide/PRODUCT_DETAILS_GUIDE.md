# Product Details Guide

**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. What is the product details page?

It shows one product: images, description, price (when visible), options/variants, quantity, and buy or quote actions.

**Why you need it:** Choose the correct options and quantity before **Add to Cart** or **Request Quote**.

**Where to find it:** Click any product name or card from search, catalogue, or homepage.

---

## 2. How to use the product page

### Step 1 — Review the product

Check images, description, stock messages, and price (or **Price on Request** / **Your Price**).

### Step 2 — Choose options

If the product has variants, select the required options. Listings may show **Select Options** first.

### Step 3 — Set quantity

Use **Quantity:** with + / − or the quantity field. The store may enforce minimum, maximum, and pack-size rules.

### Step 4 — Choose an action

| Button | When it appears | Result |
|--------|-----------------|--------|
| **Add to Cart** | Signed-in buyers, or B2C guests when prices are visible | Item goes to **Shopping Cart** |
| **Request Quote** | When quotes are enabled for your store | Item goes to **Request for Quote** |
| Wishlist (heart) | When wishlist is available | Saves or removes the product |
| **In Cart** / **View Cart** | Already at cart quantity | Opens the cart |
| **Sign In** | Action needs an account | Opens sign-in |

---

## 3. Quantity messages you may see

| Message | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Minimum Quantity Not Met** | Increase quantity to the minimum |
| **Maximum Quantity Exceeded** | Reduce quantity |
| **Invalid Quantity** | Enter a valid number |
| **Quantity limit reached** | Cart already has the maximum allowed |

---

## 4. Expected result

The product is in your cart or quote list, or saved to wishlist, with the quantity you selected.
