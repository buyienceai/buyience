# Search and Filter Guide

**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. What is search and filter?

Tools to find products by name or SKU, then narrow results with sort and price filters.

**Where to find it:** Header search field — placeholder **Search products, SKUs...** — and the search results page after you submit.

---

## 2. Header search (step by step)

1. Click **Search products, SKUs...** in the header.
2. Type a product name or SKU.
3. Pick a suggestion, or press search/submit to open full results.
4. Use **Recent Searches** if shown; clear with **Clear all**.

Suggestion actions may include **Select Options**, **Add to Cart**, **View Cart**, **Request Quote**, or **View RFQ**.

---

## 3. Search results — sort options

Exact labels:

- **Select Sort Option** (default)  
- **Name (A-Z)**  
- **Name (Z-A)**  
- **Price (Low to High)**  
- **Price (High to Low)**  
- **Top Sellers**  
- **Newest**

---

## 4. Price filter

- Min / max price controls appear when your store shows prices for your session.
- Clear filters with the clear chips / **Clear Search** controls on the page.

Category browse cards may also appear on the search page when your supplier configured them.

---

## 5. Catalogue page search

On **Product Catalog**, the on-page **Search products...** box filters the list as you type.

---

## 6. Expected result

A product list matching your query, sort, and price range.
