# Navigation Guide

**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. What is storefront navigation?

The header and footer help you move around the shop: search products, open the cart, request a quote, sign in, and reach support or policy pages.

**Where:** Top and bottom of every storefront page (the buyer **Dashboard** may hide the main shop footer).

---

## 2. Header controls

Top menu names are set by your supplier. These controls are always part of the storefront:

| Control | What it does |
|---------|----------------|
| Store **logo** | Returns to the homepage |
| Mega-menu / nav links | Categories and pages your supplier configured (some themes show **View All Categories**) |
| Search | Placeholder **Search products, SKUs...** — suggestions appear as you type |
| Cart icon | Opens **Shopping Cart** (badge shows item count when available) |
| Quote / RFQ icon | Opens **Request for Quote** when quotes are enabled for your store |
| **Dashboard** | Opens your buyer account area when you are signed in and allowed |
| Account menu | **Account Settings**, **Company Settings** (when permitted), **Analytics Dashboard** (when permitted), **Log out** |
| Guest buttons | Often Sign In / Register (Register may be hidden on B2C stores) |

### Search suggestions

When you focus search you may see:

- **Recent Searches** with **Clear all**  
- Products with **SKU**, **In stock** / **Out of stock**, optional **Pack of N**  
- Price or **Price on Request**  
- Actions such as **Select Options**, **Add to Cart**, **In Cart** / **View Cart**, **Request Quote**, **In RFQ** / **View RFQ**, or **Sign In**

---

## 3. Footer

Default column titles:

- **QUICK LINKS**  
- **ADDITIONAL LINKS**  
- **Contact Us**  
- **Follow Us** (when social links are set)  

You may also see payment logos, a country/currency selector, and a theme mode toggle. Link names in the footer are set by your supplier.

---

## 4. Other common destinations

| What you want | How to get there |
|---------------|------------------|
| Contact the store | Footer **Contact Us**, or **Support** / **Support Center** when linked |
| Policy pages | Footer **ADDITIONAL LINKS** (or similar) |
| All categories | Menu **Categories** (if linked) or homepage category tiles |
| Full catalogue | Menu **Catalog** / **Product Catalog** (if linked) |
| Sign in | Guest button or account entry → **Sign in to your account** |
| Create account | On the sign-in screen: **Create a B2B account** |
