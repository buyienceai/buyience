# FAQ and Troubleshooting

**Last updated:** November 2026

## FAQ

### Why do I see “Price on Request”?

The store hides prices for guests or for your account until sign-in / price rules allow them.

### Why is there no Add to Cart?

Common reasons:

- Product needs **Select Options** (variants)  
- Out of stock when the store tracks inventory  
- Your account is not allowed to place orders  
- The store expects **Request Quote** instead  

### Why is Request Quote missing?

Quotes may be disabled for the store, or you are on a B2C guest path that uses cart instead of quote.

### Why are Dashboard items missing?

Menus depend on your role. **Coming Soon** items (for example **Purchase Orders**) are not available yet. Ask your company admin for access to missing areas.

### The payment method I expected is missing

Only methods your supplier enabled appear. Contact the store through **Support** or footer **Contact Us**.

### Can I change the marketing website buyience.com from the storefront?

No. The storefront is your shopping portal with that supplier. Seller tools and the public marketing site are separate.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| Cannot sign in | Check email/password; use **Forgot your password?**; complete two-factor if prompted |
| Cart empty after sign-in | Guest cart may not carry over — add items again or **Import list to cart** |
| Checkout blocked | Fill required fields marked *; select shipping and payment term/method |
| Payment failed | Use **Try Again** on **Payment Failed**, or the pay link from your supplier |
| No shipping methods | Fix the address; contact the store via **Support** |
| Store not found | Use the exact store link from your supplier |
