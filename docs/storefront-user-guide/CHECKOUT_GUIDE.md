# Checkout Guide

**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. What is checkout?

Checkout is where you enter contact and address details, choose shipping and payment, then place the order. It is one page with sections (not a multi-page wizard).

**Why you need it:** The cart only holds items — checkout creates the order.

**Where to find it:** Cart → **Proceed to Checkout**.

---

## 2. Guest checkout (B2C stores that allow guests)

Sections usually include **Personal Information**, shipping address, and **Payment Method**.

### What to enter

- **First Name**, **Last Name**, email, mobile, company, password (when asked)  
- Address lines, **Country**, **State**, **City**, **Zip Code**  
- Payment option when shown: **Credit Card** / **Credit / debit card**, **PayPal**, or **Razorpay**

### Buttons

| Button | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Back to Cart** | Return to the cart |
| **Place Order** | Submit the order (**Processing...** while working) |

---

## 3. Signed-in / B2B checkout

### Company Information (hidden on B2C)

- **Company Name** (required)  
- **Department** when shown  

### Contact and addresses

1. Fill contact information.  
2. Choose saved **Billing Address** / **Shipping Address**, or enter a new address.  
3. Keep billing the same as shipping when that option is offered.  
4. Pick from address suggestions when they appear.

### Purchase order (when shown)

| Option | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **No PO Required** | Continue without a PO |
| **Link to Existing PO** | Attach an existing PO |
| **Create New PO** | Create a PO for this order |

### Special instructions / approval

- Enter special instructions when the field is shown.  
- Click **Submit for Approval** if your company requires approval before ordering.

### Shipping

1. Select or enter your shipping address.  
2. Choose a shipping method from the options returned for that address.  
3. You may see free shipping, a charge, or **Shipping Not Available**.  
4. Review shipping and tax on the order summary when shown.

### Payment terms and methods

Required labels:

- **Payment terms** *  
- **Payment method** *  

Options depend on your supplier. Examples you may see:

- **Credit / debit card**, **PayPal**, **Razorpay**  
- **Pay by invoice (no payment now)**  
- **Pay online later, from your invoice**  
- Other offline methods with on-screen instructions  

A note may explain that payment is taken in the store’s base currency even if you browse in another currency.

### Place the order

| Button | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Place Order** | Submit the order |
| **Confirm Your Order** / **Confirm Order** | Confirm in the dialog when shown |
| **Processing...** | Wait — do not click again |

---

## 4. After you place the order

| What you see | Meaning |
|--------------|---------|
| **Order Placed Successfully!** | Order created — use **View Orders** or **Continue Shopping** |
| **Payment not completed** | Order exists but payment still needed |
| **Payment Failed** | Use **Try Again**, or **Continue shopping** / **Back to orders** |
| Invoice pay page | **Invoice summary**, **Balance due**, pay or **Download** |

---

## 5. Expected result

An order is created. You either finish on the success screen, or continue in the payment window if payment is due now.

### Mistakes to avoid

- Assuming every payment method exists — only what your store shows is available.  
- Leaving required fields (marked *) empty.  
- Choosing shipping before the address is complete.
