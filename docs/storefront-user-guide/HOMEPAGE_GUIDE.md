# Homepage Guide

**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. What is the homepage?

It is the first screen of your supplier’s Buyience storefront — the branded shop you open from the link they shared with you.

**Why you need it:** It introduces the store and gives you quick paths to products, search, cart, quotes, and your account.

**Where to find it:** Open your store link, or click the store **logo** in the header from any page.

---

## 2. What you may see

Homepage sections are set by your supplier. You may see any of these:

| Section | What customers see |
|---------|-------------------|
| Hero / image slider | Large promotional banners |
| Categories | Tiles to browse product groups |
| Product grid or product slider | Product cards with prices or **Price on Request** |
| Image or text banners | Marketing messages |
| Testimonials | Customer quotes |
| Newsletter | Email signup (if enabled) |
| FAQs | Expandable questions |

Exact titles and images come from **your store** — they are not the same on every Buyience shop.

---

## 3. How to use the homepage

1. Open the store URL your supplier shared.
2. Use the header search (**Search products, SKUs...**) or category tiles to find products.
3. Click a product card to open the product details.
4. Use header icons for **Cart**, account / **Sign in**, and (when available) Request Quote. Wishlist hearts appear on product cards and product details, not as a separate header icon.

---

## 4. Expected result

You land on a branded homepage and can move into browsing, search, cart, or sign-in using the on-screen controls.
