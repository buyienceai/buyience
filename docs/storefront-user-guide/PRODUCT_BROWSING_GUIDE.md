# Product Browsing Guide

**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. What is product browsing?

Ways to find products in the storefront: homepage sections, **Categories**, **Product Catalog**, category pages, and brand pages.

**Why you need it:** You must find the right item before you can add it to cart or request a quote.

---

## 2. Where to find products

| Place | How to open it |
|-------|----------------|
| Homepage | Product grids, sliders, or category tiles |
| **Categories** | Header or footer link (when your supplier added it) |
| Category page | Click a category name or tile |
| Brand page | Click a brand name when shown |
| **Product Catalog** | Header or footer **Catalog** link (when added) |
| Search | Header **Search products, SKUs...** |

---

## 3. Product Catalog

Page title: **Product Catalog**.

1. Open **Catalog** from the menu (if linked).
2. Use **Search products...** on the page; clear with **Clear search** if needed.
3. Switch **grid** or **list** view with the view icons.
4. Read **Results: … of …**; empty state shows **No products found.**
5. Click a product card to open details.

**Note:** The catalogue page has search and view modes. Category pages have the fuller **Filters** and **Sort By** sidebar.

---

## 4. Category pages

1. Open a category from the menu, homepage tiles, or **Categories**.
2. Use **Filters**: **Clear All Filters**, **Price Range**, and any attribute filters shown.
3. Use **Sort By**: **Name (A-Z)**, **Name (Z-A)**, **Price (Low to High)**, **Price (High to Low)**, **Top Sellers**, **Newest**.
4. Switch grid/list view.
5. Open a product for details.

---

## 5. Product cards — buttons you may see

| Button / state | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Add to Cart** / **Adding...** | Adds a simple product to the cart |
| **Select Options** | Opens the product page (variants or options needed) |
| **Request Quote** | Adds the item toward a quote request |
| Heart / wishlist | Save or remove from wishlist |
| **Price on Request** | Price hidden until your store rules allow it |
| **Your Price** | Your negotiated customer price |
| **In Cart** / **View Cart** | Item already in cart |
| **Sign In** | You must sign in before this action |

---

## 6. Expected result

You locate a product and open its details, or add it to cart / quote using the button shown for your session.
