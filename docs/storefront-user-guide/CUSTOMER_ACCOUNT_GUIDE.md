# Customer Account Guide

**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. What is the customer account?

Your sign-in, registration, and buyer **Dashboard** — where you manage orders, quotes, invoices, and returns for this store.

**Why you need it:** Many prices, quotes, and order history require a signed-in account.

---

## 2. Sign in (step by step)

**Where:** Guest header button, or the link to **Sign in to your account**.

| Element | Label |
|---------|-------|
| Title | **Sign in to your account** |
| Email | **Email address** — **Enter your email** |
| Password | **Password** — **Enter your password** |
| Submit | **Sign in** / **Signing in...** |
| Forgot | **Forgot your password?** |
| New user | **New to our platform?** → **Create a B2B account** |
| Help | **Need Help?** / **Support Center** |

1. Enter email and password.  
2. Click **Sign in**.  
3. If asked for two-factor, open **Enter Verification Code** (or recovery code), then **Verify Code** / **Verify Recovery Code**. Use **Back to Login** if you need to restart.

---

## 3. Create an account

**Where:** Sign-in screen → **Create a B2B account**.

Titles may say **Create your account** / **Create Your Account**. Submit with **Register** / **Registering...**.

Fields are set by your supplier. Common groups:

- Business: Company Name, Tax ID, Tax Type, Industry, Department, Address, Country, State, City, ZIP  
- User: First/Last Name, Email, Phone, Job Title, Password, Confirm Password, **Generate Strong Password**  

After success you may see **Go to Login** and email verification instructions.

---

## 4. Forgot password

1. On sign-in, click **Forgot your password?**  
2. Open **Reset Password**, enter email, click **Send Reset Link** / **Sending...**.  
3. Check email (**Check Your Email**), then **Back to Login** or **Sign up** as needed.

---

## 5. Account menu (header)

When signed in:

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Account Settings** | Your profile / account details |
| **Company Settings** | Company settings (when permitted) |
| **Analytics Dashboard** | Analytics (when permitted) |
| **Log out** | Sign out of the store |

---

## 6. Buyer Dashboard

**Where:** Header → **Dashboard** (or account entry to the dashboard).

Sidebar group label: **Dashboard**.

### Quick actions

| Control | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Create Quote** | Open **Request for Quote** |
| **Back to Store** | Return to the shop |

### Menu items

| Menu | Purpose | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| **Overview** | Account overview / analytics | May be limited by role |
| **Analytics** | Deeper analytics | May be limited by role |
| **Orders** | Purchases you placed in this store | Not the same as **Purchase Orders** |
| **Quotes** | Your quote requests | |
| **Invoices** | Invoices and balances | |
| **Returns** | Return requests | |
| **Saved Lists** | Product list templates | May be limited by role |
| **Reorder** | Quick reorder hub | May be limited by role |
| **Purchase Orders** | Supplier-style PO tools | Often **Coming Soon** — separate from **Orders** |

If a menu is missing, your company admin has not given you that access. Items under Advanced (for example Approvals, Budget, Contracts, Bulk Orders) are not available in the live menu for most customers.

---

## 7. Expected result

You are signed in and can open the Dashboard areas your role allows.
