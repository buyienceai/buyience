# Buyience Admin & Seller User Manual

**Who this is for:** Store admins, business owners, and team members who use the Buyience dashboard day to day.

**What this is:** A practical how-to guide — not developer documentation.

**Dashboard app (reference):** Buyience / Nova Core admin at `app.buyience.com` (source: `buyience-app-dashboard`)

**Marketing website:** `buyience.com` — product pages and blogs are marketing content. Your live shop catalogue is managed in the **dashboard** and appears on **your storefront**, not as edits to the marketing website.

---

## Important: Admin vs Seller in Buyience

| Term in this guide | What it means in the real product |
|--------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Admin** | A user with full (or wide) permission to run the store: catalogue, orders, settings, users, billing. |
| **Seller / store operator** | Anyone on your team who sells and fulfils through this dashboard (quotes, orders, stock). In chats and quotes, “seller” means **your store**, not a separate marketplace vendor. |
| **Marketplace seller portal** | **Not available.** There is no separate seller centre with commissions and payouts. |

Everyone uses the **same dashboard**. What you see depends on the **role and permissions** your admin assigns.
