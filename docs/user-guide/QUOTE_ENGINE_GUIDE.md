# Quote Engine Guide

**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. What is the Quote Engine?

It is where your team creates and manages B2B quotes, negotiates with buyers, and handles RFQ invitations.

**Why you need it:** Many B2B deals start with a quote, not a cart checkout.

**Where:** Left menu → **Quote Engine**

| Menu item | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **View All** | Quote list and filters |
| **Digital Sales Room** (DSR) | Buyer/seller chat inbox |
| **RFQ Invitations** | Shareable invitation links for buyers to request quotes |
| **Create Quote** | Start a new B2B quote |

---

## 2. Create a new quote (step by step)

1. Open **Quote Engine** → **Create Quote** (page title often **New B2B Quote**).
2. Follow the steps: **Customer** → **Products** → **Terms** → **Review**.

### Customer step

1. Open **Customer Setup**.
2. Select or enter the buyer/customer for this quote.
3. Continue to Products.

### Products step

1. Search with **Search products by name or SKU...**
2. Click **Add** for each line.
3. Confirm items appear under **Added to Quote**.
4. Adjust quantities/prices as the form allows.
5. Continue to Terms.

### Terms step

Fill **Quote Terms** as needed:

| Field | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Expected Delivery Date** | When you plan to deliver |
| **Quote Valid Until** | Expiry date for the offer |
| **Payment Terms** | How/when the buyer should pay |
| **Priority** | Internal priority for the deal |

### Review step

1. Check customer, lines, and terms.
2. Helper text may say **Confirm & send**.
3. Click **Create Quote**.

**Expected result:** The quote is created and appears in **View All**.

---

## 3. Manage quotes from the list

**Where:** Quote Engine → **View All**

### Header actions

| Button | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Create Quote** | Start a new quote |
| **Export** | Export the list (as available) |
| **More Filters** | Narrow the list |
| **Refresh** | Reload data |

### Tabs

**All Quotes**, **Pending Approval**, **Negotiation**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Converted**, **Archived**

Use tabs to focus on the stage you care about.

### Row / bulk actions

| Action | Purpose | Reversible? |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **Archive Quote** / **Archive Selected** | Hide from active lists | Usually yes via **Restore** |
| **Restore Quote** / **Restore Selected** | Bring back archived quotes | N/A |
| **Re-quote** | Start a new version from an existing quote | Creates a new quote flow |

---

## 4. Send, accept, reject, and chat

On quote detail (labels as shown in the product):

| Button | When to use | Result |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| **Confirm & Send** | Ready to send to the buyer | Buyer receives/opens the quote per your setup |
| **Accept** | Mark accepted (when available to your role) | Moves toward approved/converted |
| **Reject Quote** | Decline the deal | Lands in Rejected |
| **Negotiation Chat** | Continue discussion | Opens chat context |
| **PDF Preview** | Review printable PDF | Opens preview page |

**Mistake to avoid:** Sending before prices and validity dates are correct.

## 5. Digital Sales Room (DSR)

**Where:** Quote Engine → **Digital Sales Room** (also called **DSR**; inbox / quote chats)

1. Open the inbox.
2. Select a conversation.
3. Reply to the buyer in the thread.
4. Keep negotiation in one place instead of scattered email.

**Benefit:** One current version of the conversation next to the quote process.

---

## 6. RFQ Invitations

**Where:** Quote Engine → **RFQ Invitations**

### Create an invitation

1. Click **Create invitation**.
2. Follow the steps; use **Continue** as prompted.
3. Click **Generate invitation**.
4. On **Share link**, copy the link for your buyer.

### List actions

| Action | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **View** | Open invitation details |
| **Copy link** | Share again |
| **Revoke** | Disable the invitation |

**Expected result:** Buyer can open the link and request a quote (per your invitation setup).

---

## 7. Floor margin during quoting

If your store uses **Floor Margin**, a price that is too low may need approval before send.

- Follow on-screen approval prompts.
- Do not bypass by editing outside the system — you lose the audit trail.

---

## 8. Common mistakes

1. Creating a quote with no customer selected.  
2. Forgetting **Quote Valid Until**.  
3. Archiving an active negotiation by mistake (use **Restore**).  
4. Negotiating only in email while the DSR chat is empty.
