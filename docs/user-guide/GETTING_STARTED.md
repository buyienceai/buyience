# Getting Started with the Buyience Dashboard

## What is the Buyience dashboard?

It is the control centre for your B2B (and retail) store: products, prices, stock, quotes, orders, customers, and storefront settings.

You open it at your company login address (commonly **app.buyience.com** or your tenant login link). You do **not** manage your live catalogue by editing the marketing site buyience.com.

---

## Before you begin

You need:

1. A login email and password from your company admin (or from Buyience onboarding).
2. Access to the correct store / subdomain (your company account).
3. A modern browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox recommended).

If two-factor authentication (2FA) is enabled, keep your authenticator app or recovery codes ready.

---

## Step 1 — Sign in

1. Go to your Buyience login page.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Click the login / sign-in button.
4. If asked for a **two-factor code**, open your authenticator app, enter the code, and continue.
5. After a successful login you arrive on the **Dashboard** (or Getting Started, if your store is still being set up).

### What happens after login?

- Your name and permissions load.
- The left menu shows only areas you are allowed to use.
- If your subscription payment failed, you may be limited to billing or payment pages until that is fixed.

### Common mistakes

- Using the marketing website login by mistake — use the **app** login.
- Caps Lock on when typing the password.
- Entering an old 2FA code that has already expired (codes refresh every ~30 seconds).

---

## Step 2 — Learn the layout

| Area | What it is for |
|------|----------------|
| **Left menu** | Main sections: Dashboard, Catalogue/Products, Orders, Quote Engine, Inventory, Customers, Discounts, Settings |
| **Top / header** | Account access, search (where available), notifications |
| **Main panel** | Lists, forms, and detail pages |

Menu names can appear as **Catalogue** (older menu) or **Products** (newer sidebar). Both lead to the same product tools.

---

## Step 3 — Complete Getting Started (new stores)

If you see **Getting Started** in the menu:

1. Open it from the left menu.
2. Follow each setup step (store details, basic preferences).
3. Finish the checklist so your store is ready to sell.

Some B2B rule cards may show **Coming Soon** — skip those until they become available.

---

## Step 4 — Recommended first tasks (admin)

1. **Store Settings** — confirm store name, branding, and regional formats.  
2. **Categories & Brands** — create the structure you need before many products.  
3. **Add a simple product** — practice the Add Product flow.  
4. **Payment Methods / Shipping / Tax** — under Settings → Business Operations (as permitted).  
5. **Invite a teammate** — Users & Roles (admin only).

---

## Step 5 — Recommended first tasks (seller / operator)

1. Open **Dashboard** and note open orders and quotes.  
2. Review **Products** list — know what is Published vs Draft.  
3. Check **Inventory** for low-stock alerts.  
4. Open **Quote Engine** if you sell by quote.  
5. Process any **New** or **Pending** orders.

---

## Permissions in plain language

| If you cannot see a menu… | Likely reason |
|---------------------------|---------------|
| Products / Catalogue missing | No catalogue permission |
| Settings → Plan & Billing missing | No billing permission |
| User Management missing | Not an admin for users |

Ask your store admin to update **Roles & Permissions**. Do not share passwords to “borrow” access.

---

## Sign out

1. Open your account / profile menu.  
2. Choose **Logout** / **Sign out** (or open the logout page if your theme provides one).  
3. Close the browser on shared computers.
